📌How often ....? (Ne sıklıkla...?)

-*- Normal Sıklık Zarfları -*-

-How often do you have a breakfast?

I always have a breakfast.

-How often does Ayşe play volleyball?

She sometimes plays volleyball.

💡 Times(Zamanlar)

minute : dakika
hour : saat
day : gün
week : hafta
weekday : haftaiçi
weekend : haftasonu
month : ay
year : yıl

-*- Yıldızlı Sıklık Zarfları -*-

-How often do you go to school?

I go to school 5 times a week.

-How often does Ali do origami?

He does origami twice a month.
